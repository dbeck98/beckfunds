The Fund

BIG Growth Fund I, LP

Private real estate — Texas Hill Country  ·  Vintage 2026

Strategy

BIG Growth Fund I acquires, develops, and repositions residential and hospitality real estate across the Texas Hill Country — one of the fastest-growing corridors in the country, with structural supply constraints in the markets we know street by street.

The fund is built on a simple discipline: every dollar is either working in a project we control, or earning in an insured interest-bearing account while it waits. Nothing sits idle, and every investor can see exactly where their capital is — project by project, account by account — at any time, on this portal.
